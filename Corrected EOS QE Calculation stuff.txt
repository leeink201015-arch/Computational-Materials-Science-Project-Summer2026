EOS fitting note:
The initial bulk modulus (B0) was incorrectly reported as 1302.59 GPa as I accidentally used a Ry/Bohr³-to-GPa conversion factor while the fitted volume was expressed in Å³. Since the Birch-Murnaghan fit uses energies in Ry and volumes in Å³, B0 is obtained in Ry/Å³. By using the correct conversion (1 Ry/Å³ ≈ 2179.87 GPa), I was able to get a far more meaningful and reasonable result here: B0 ≈ 193.0 GPa.

The equilibrium lattice constant and other fitted parameters were unaffected by this unit-conversion correction so that's good.
